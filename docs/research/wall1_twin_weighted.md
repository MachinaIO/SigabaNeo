# A least-determinant witness for a bad twin extension

At fixed 1% tour slack and exact terminal SVP, the relative improvement certified here over the **unrounded** Part 88 radius is at least $e^{-584}$ at width 406 and $e^{-734}$ at width 506. The improvement disappears at eight decimal places. The one-hyperplane exchange condition used below can itself certify a relative gain of at most $e^{-170}$ at either width. These bounds concern a determinant relaxation of the bad event on a covolume-one Haar lattice of rank 1023.

## Selection and an exchange obstruction

Put $k=n-b$, $r=k+1$, $m=n-r$, and let $a$ be the Part 88 upper twin factor, $T=a^{k/(k-1)}$. A bad passing output with head $g<\rho$ has a primitive extension of determinant at most $(Tg)^r$. Select a primitive rank-$r$ sublattice of **globally least determinant**, with measurable ties broken in a fixed enumeration in a measurable lattice chart. A minimizer exists by discreteness of the exterior lattice. The selected count under cutoff $(T\rho)^r$ is zero or one, and dominates the bad-event indicator, simultaneously over passing bases at the given width. This deliberately relaxes passing *before* selection. It makes a determinant-decreasing replacement decisive even when that replacement fails the twin tests. The real-arithmetic, block-subroutine and Hermite-cap qualifications are those of [Part 88](wall1_li_walter.md).

Consider a marked candidate $P$ of determinant $D$. Write $H=D^{-1/r}P$, $J=D^{1/m}\pi_{P^\perp}L$. Take a shortest primitive normal in $H^*$ of length $t$ and let $W$ be its kernel in $P$. The step of $P/W$ has length $g_0=D^{1/r}/t$. Lift a shortest vector of $J$ of length $z$ to $L$, then translate by $P$ to leave a component along $P/W$ of magnitude at most $g_0/2$ after projection off $W$. The sublattice generated by $W$ and this lift has determinant at most
$$
 \det W\sqrt{D^{-2/m}z^2+g_0^2/4}.
$$
Its saturation only reduces its determinant. It differs from $P$ because its projection off $P$ is nonzero. Consequently the selected candidate satisfies
$$
 t z\ \ge\ (\sqrt3/2)D^{1/r+1/m}.
$$
In fact it survives every rank-$r$ replacement sharing a hyperplane. Conversely $tz\ge D^{1/r+1/m}$ suffices to survive every such exchange: the common saturated hyperplane has step in $P$ at most $D^{1/r}/t$, while every replacing vector projects off $P$ with length at least $D^{-1/m}z$. The common hyperplane here is the intersection lattice for two primitive planes. These statements concern one exchange at fixed rank. Global minimality has further constraints.

## Haar integral and certified gain

Use the first moment of candidates below cutoff surviving the displayed necessary product inequality. Under primitive incidence $H^*$ and $J$ are independent unimodular Haar shapes, with radial intensity $nK_r D^{n-1}dD$. Thus this first moment has a nonconstant survival factor even though one imposes no terminal shortest-vector test. Write $R$ for the Part 88 solution $K_r(TR)^{nr}=.01$, $q=nr$, $d=TR$ and $p=1/r+1/m$. In the radial band $2^{-1/n}d^r<D<d^r$, take
$$
 x=(4/V_r)^{1/r},\qquad y=(\sqrt3/2)(2^{-1/n}d^r)^p/x.
$$
The simultaneous events $t\le x$, $z\le y$ preclude selection. For the count of primitive unoriented lines in a ball of volume $V$, the mean is $h=V/(2\zeta(j))$ and the second moment is $h+h^2$ in these dimensions. Cauchy–Schwarz gives hit probability at least $h/(1+h)$. Using $\zeta(j)<2$, the two probabilities are at least $1/2$ and $h_0/(1+h_0)$ respectively, $h_0=V_m y^m/4$. The band contains half the mass at $R$. At $R(1+v)$ the first moment divided by .01 is therefore at most
$$
 (1+v)^q-h_0/[4(1+h_0)] .
$$
For $v=e^{-584},e^{-734}$ respectively the script certifies $v<h_0/[16q(1+h_0)]$. Since $(1+v)^q\le e^{qv}\le1+2qv$ for these values, Markov proves the table. This use of the second moment is confined to lines in each marked shape. No multiplicity of bad extensions conditional on existence is assumed. [Primitive incidence and Rogers normalization](wall1_selfdual_sharpen.md)

For the limitation let $u=e^{-170}$. Through $D=[d(1+u)]^r$, the cutoffs $x'=0.65V_r^{-1/r}$ and $y'=0.65V_m^{-1/m}$ satisfy $x'y'>D^p$. Except on a set of marked shapes of probability at most $s=(0.65^r+0.65^m)/2$ by Siegel, **all** one-hyperplane exchanges survive. Their count has mean divided by .01 at least $(1+u)^q(1-s)>1$, using $qu(1-s)>s$. Even the full one-exchange test alone thus saturates. This upper limit does not apply to global selection or to its combination with passing tests.

[Part 90](wall1_selfdual_sharpen.md) brackets a different mean, of distinct passing bad extensions. The present strict gain exceeds its explicit $e^{-800}/8$ lower certificate. The ordering of the two *optimal* Markov radii is unresolved. Normalizing weights over passing extensions as suggested in option (b) would only give the fraction that are bad. A cover of the bad event requires total weight at least one **on the bad witnesses** or a proved domination via a separate relaxed count as above. The completion multiplicities of [Part 83](wall1_basis_multiplicity.md) and the exact BKZ local rigidity of [Part 85](wall1_canonical_bad_basis.md) supply no such lower bound for distinct twin extensions. The reported gains use no Poisson or GSA assumption. GSA enters only the comparison target. The Haar argument gives no transfer to the finite-modulus ensemble.

## Interval reproduction

Run from the repository root. The script imports the Part 90 row definition, including the Part 86 interval setup and audited cap inputs. It compares small gains symbolically to avoid rounding $1+e^{-734}$ to one.

```python
from pathlib import Path
src = Path("docs/research/wall1_selfdual_sharpen.md").read_text()
exec(src.split("\n```python\n")[1].split("u=I.mpf")[0])
for b, exponent, floor8, gaplo, gaphi in [
    (406,584,12531282,".24388",".24390"),
    (506,734,36188705,".45442",".45444")
]:
    R, ratio, T, _ = row(b, ".01")
    r, m = n-b+1, b-1
    q = n*r
    p = 1/I.mpf(r)+1/I.mpf(m)
    d = T*R
    x = (4/ball(r))**(1/I.mpf(r))
    y = I.sqrt(3)/2 * I.exp((r*I.ln(d)-I.ln(2)/n)*p)/x
    h = ball(m)*y**m/4
    v, u = I.exp(-I.mpf(exponent)), I.exp(-I.mpf(170))
    assert v < h/(16*q*(1+h))
    c = I.mpf(".65")
    xy = c**2 * I.exp(-I.ln(ball(r))/r-I.ln(ball(m))/m)
    assert I.ln(xy) > r*p*(I.ln(d)+u)
    s = (c**r+c**m)/2
    assert q*u*(1-s)>s
    assert floor8 < 10**8*R and 10**8*R*(1+u)<floor8+1
    target = R/ratio
    assert I.mpf(gaplo)<target-R*(1+u) and target-R<I.mpf(gaphi)
    print(b, floor8, exponent, I.ln(h))
```
