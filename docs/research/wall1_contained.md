# Capture when the selected prefix contains the plant

Let the exact BKZ width be $\beta$, the lattice rank be $d$, and $k=d-\beta$ be the rank preceding the terminal block. Write $W_j$ for the lattice generated by the first $j$ output rows. Since these rows extend to a basis, $L\cap\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}W_j=W_j$. For a primitive plant $u$, let $C$ be the event that $u\in W_k$ and no output row equals $\pm u$. This is the contained failure event in [CaptureStrata](../../SigabaNeo/Lib/Lattices/Research/Wall1/CaptureStrata.lean). The bounds below concern its **joint** probability, with no division by the probability of containment.

## A shorter ratio profile for the contained event

For $k=0,1$, $C$ is empty by primitivity. Otherwise set $a=(k-\beta)_+=(d-2\beta)_+$ and $T=\|u\|\exp(P_a)$ with the natural-log ratio constants of [Part 7](ratio_unique.md), including $P_0=0$. Every fully BKZ-reduced basis satisfies
$$
 C\quad\Longrightarrow\quad b_0\in W_k\setminus\mathbb Zu,\qquad \|b_0\|\le T .
$$

Indeed, truncate the basis at rank $k$. Every required projected block in the truncation is a right truncation of an original block and still contains its shortest first projected row. Thus this is a fully reduced basis of $W_k$ of width $\min(k,\beta)$. Write $t$ for the largest zero-based index of a nonzero coefficient of $u$ on containment, and put $j=\min(t,a)$. The projection of $u$ past the first $j$ rows is nonzero and belongs to the block starting at $j$: if $j=t$ only row $t$ survives, and otherwise the window reaches the end of $W_k$. Hence $\|u\|\ge g_j\ge \|b_0\|e^{-P_j}$ by [Part 6](wall1_prefix.md). Monotonicity gives $P_j\le P_a$ (the recurrence through offset $a$ uses width $\beta$ whenever $a>0$). Finally, any basis row on the plant line is $\pm u$. This proves the claim. In particular, for $k\le\beta$ a unique shortest pair in the prefix suffices by itself. The same argument in this range applies to any output with an exact first primal block of width $\beta$, including a checked slide basis: on containment the first row belongs to $W_k$ and has length at most $\|u\|$. The longer-prefix proof needs overlapping projected blocks.

Fix the error and secret in the [plain planted ensemble](usvp_planted.md), write $B$ for their largest absolute coordinate, and let $V_d$ be the unit ball volume. Under $T\sqrt{1+B^2}<q$, uniformly over all selections of a fully reduced basis,
$$
 \Pr_A(C)\le\min\{1,\ q^{-m}V_d(T+\sqrt d/2)^d\}.
$$
Indeed the preceding witness is an ambient off-line vector within the fixed radius $T$, so the planted finite-radius theorem applies directly. This pays the ratio penalty only for the excess of prefix rank over block width. At the Part 7 plain shape $d=701$, $m=188$, $\beta=406$, the prefix has rank 295, so there is no penalty. With the independent CBD planting of that table, its $2^{-64}$ norm-tail and $2^{-64}$ off-line-vector bounds give $\Pr(C)\le2^{-63}$. This controls one stratum only. Under the registered slide shape $d=2\beta$, the first primal block is the whole prefix, so the same finite-radius inequality holds with $T=\|u\|$.

## A unique prefix minimum can be missed at the very next rank

Take width two in rank five. Use the following first three rows, padded with two zeros, followed by $20e_4,20e_5$:
$$
 b_0=(20,0,0),\qquad b_1=(9,18,0),\qquad b_2=(9,8,17).
$$
The integral lattice generated by these rows has a primitive mark $u=b_2-b_1=(0,-10,17)$ of squared length 389. All rows are size reduced: the nonzero Gram–Schmidt coefficients are $9/20,9/20,8/18$. In the first pair a coefficient of magnitude at least two on the second generator gives height at least 36. For coefficient $\pm1$ the minimum squared norm is $9^2+18^2=405>400$. In the next projected pair the generators are $(18,0),(8,17)$, with corresponding squared norm $353>324$ and double height $34>18$. The remaining projected pairs are orthogonal with ordered lengths $(17,20)$ and $(20,20)$. Thus all exact BKZ windows pass.

For any vector of squared length at most 389 the last two coefficients vanish. The coefficient on $b_2$ has magnitude at most one by its height 17. When it vanishes the first pair has minimum squared length 400. Otherwise negate to make it one. The second coordinate $8+18h$ must have absolute value at most 10, leaving $h=0,-1$. For $h=0$ the first coordinate is $9\bmod 20$, giving squared length at least $434$. For $h=-1$ only first coordinate zero fits, giving exactly $u$. Thus $\pm u$ are the unique shortest pair even in the ambient lattice. Neither occurs as a row, and the rank-three prefix kills the projection. This realizes $k=\beta+1$ in the smallest width considered, in a general integral lattice. It supplies no frequency estimate in the plain planted ensemble.

For longer prefixes the quantitative hypothesis is a gap out to $T$, stronger than uniqueness alone. When the finite-radius estimate is vacuous, the missing information is how often the selected prefix contains both the plant and the independent first-row witness. The example rules out deducing capture from prefix uniqueness and local reduction alone. The noncontained same-line and competing-line probabilities remain separate requirements for a success bound.
