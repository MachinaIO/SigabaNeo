## A conditional count for the mixed plane

Use $D_i=\det M_{i-1}=V_{506}g_i^{506}$ for the **incoming** determinant, $V_n$ for unit ball volume, $a=1352/\sqrt3$, $R=\sqrt{\sqrt2 a}$, $B=52/\sqrt3$, and $u=\sqrt{5a/4}$. On a horizontal win, project off the pre-draw designated shortest vector $v$ of length $s\le R$. Let $N$ be the rank-504 projected old lattice. In the residual mixed case $a_2(N)>a$. Its hit plane has primitive height-zero kernel generated by $x$, of length $\sqrt a<t=\|x\|\le B$. The positive generator of its height image is $k$. After reduction along $x$ a fresh generator has length at most $r(t)=\sqrt{(a/t)^2+t^2/4}$. After also projecting off $x$, its length is at most $a/t$, with the same $k$. The function $r$ decreases on this interval. [Part 194, first-hit lengths](wall1_z1_horizontal.md#first-hit-lengths-and-the-mixed-plane-estimate)

For projected window rank $n$, let
$$
 F_n(r;K,h)=1.423\left[2V_{n-1}\sum_{k=h}^K(r^2-k^2)^{(n-1)/2}
             +V_n(r^2-K(K+2))^{n/2}\right],\qquad 1\le h\le K+1 .
$$
Dividing by the projected old determinant bounds the conditional probability of a positive-height candidate with height at least $h$. Each tested height is below $q=3329$ and fixes a uniform projected mark. For heights above $K$, translation by $-K$ takes distinct labels into a positive half-ball with squared radius reduced by at least $K(K+2)$. This counts the original labels at their original weight. The Part 161 half-ball coefficient decreases with radius and is below 1.423 at 26.5 in ranks 503–506. The verifier checks the smallest translated and horizontal radii each time the formula is used. [Part 161, radial bound](wall1_band_plane_hits.md) · [Part 215, height count](wall1_fresh_mark_extension.md)

**Line-count lemma.** For $b>\sqrt a$ there are at most $L(b)$ unoriented primitive lines of $N$ of length at most $b$, where $2L(b)$ is any spherical code bound in dimension 504 at maximum inner product $\eta(b)=\sqrt{1-a^2/b^4}$. Indeed the area of any independent pair exceeds $a$, even before saturation. After normalizing both signs of every primitive representative, distinct nonantipodal representatives have absolute inner product below $\eta(b)$. Antipodal pairs have inner product $-1$.

The verifier computes a finite rational certificate for each of 42 endpoints. For normalized Gegenbauer polynomials $P_j(1)=1$ and harmonic dimensions $m_j$, it takes a rational $z\ge\eta(b)$, constructs $Q$ and $V=(1+X)^e(X-z)Q$ with nonnegative Gegenbauer coefficients, and checks $f_0=\sum_j Q_jV_j/m_j>0$. For $f=QV$, positivity of the spherical kernels and their products gives pair sum at least $f_0 N_c^2$ on $N_c$ points. Off the diagonal $f\le0$ on the code, so $L(b)=f(1)/(2f_0)$ suffices. This is the polynomial certificate from the repository applied directly to a finite set of old lines. ## Arithmetic and a reachable spacing counterexample

For frozen space $F$, write $p_F=\det(F\cap\mathbb Z^N)^2$, $d_F=\dim_{\mathbb F_q}(K/M)$, $K=\pi_{F^\perp}\mathbb Z^N$. The Part 212 regularity event gives **squared** covolume $p_F\ge2^{99}$ at freezes 395–406 and mark rank at least 115 there. For a fixed prescribed point of height between one and $q-1$ the mass in the unprojected mark group is at most $q^{-115}$. Forcing $h$ old lines can cost up to $h$ in rank. In either case the full preimage grid has covolume equal to the projected old determinant divided by the projected group order. These factors cancel in the volume count. A wholly old triple at its hit draw has no equation on that mark. [Part 212, section and projected determinant identities](wall1_integrality_bound.md#which-gram-determinant-is-integral)

Integrality does give an exact crossing test. Write $V\supset F$ for the preimage of the winner line and $T\supset V$ for the preimage of the triple. The wholly old test is $3q^{2(d_V-d_T)}p_T\le1352^2p_V$, with the incidence of the designated line retained. For a saturated parent lift plane of area $A>a$, $3p_FA^2$ is integral, so $A^2-a^2\ge1/(3p_F)$. A degree-one lift exists by lifting a basis of the saturated quotient plane into the old triple and adjoining the winner as a third basis vector. For its projection to cross the cutoff the squared component of the unit winner in the lift plane must be at least $1-a^2/A^2\ge1/(1352^2p_F+1)$. A *lower* bound on $p_F$ gives no uniform positive lower bound from this last expression. On an incoming history the entire test is already fixed. The probability of the Part 212 exceptional event is below $10^{-748}$. The lookback and mixed charges above include it.

## Terminal accounting

Let $B_o$ include both gates, the residual exclusions and a wholly old first hit, with only the terminal area test removed. Let $S$ be survival of its tie-broken plane through draw 406, by projection and saturation. Any selection outside its real plane contracts area and preserves rank two. A horizontal winner in its plane kills the trace. Write $A_{406}$ for the terminal area hit. Hence the wholly old terminal mass is exactly
$$
 \Pr(B_o\cap S)+\Pr(B_o\cap S^c\cap A_{406}).
$$
Both terms are covered whenever the **creation** is charged in the lookback table. For a first hit on draw 406 the second term is empty. For earlier uncharged histories a replacement can be old when it first qualifies. Its existence pins no prescribed continuation mark. Part 208's example and the prefix here have different reachability properties, and neither supplies a positive gated replacement probability. [Part 208, trace and replacement](wall1_z1_terminal_law.md#exact-criterion-for-losing-the-first-plane)

Summing the unrestricted mixed charges on 403–405, the restricted charges on 406 using height at least 5, the small-scale visits through 406, and the seven wholly old rows **before rounding** gives a majorant in $(6.42610,6.42611)\cdot10^{-11}$. More than $7.0139\cdot10^{-12}$ remains for the uncovered events. For scale, the unrestricted draw-406 line-count majorant alone is over 351 times this balance. The factor measures the cost of the estimate. On an uncharged incoming old history the exact one-step probability before the remaining exclusions and continuation is $I_i w_i$, where $I_i$ is the indicator of the triple test and $w_i$ is the horizontal win fraction. Its history average, with the survival and replacement weights, is the missing old incidence estimate. No estimate within the balance is proved for the remaining mixed region or for the two terminal terms on uncharged old histories.
