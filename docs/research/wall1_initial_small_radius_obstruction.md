# The initial restart void at the proposed fixed small radius

For the actual **unprojected initial restart law**, the void at
R_min=sqrt((1.10*32.42764)^2-1) is uniformly almost one. Thus fixed-physical-radius
projection contraction does not supply the small initial moment needed by
[Smoothed void potential](wall1_smoothed_void_potential.md). Starting that
potential later, for example at draw 129, leaves a different selected-distribution
initialization problem; the lower bound here is not asserted at draw 129.

## Authoritative initial law and its mark group

The marginal restart in [Selected scaling](wall1_selected_scaling.md) starts
with m=400 output coordinates and j=105 input coordinates, retained rank 505:

    L_A={(x,y) in Z^400 x Z^105 : x=A y mod q},  q=3329,

where A is uniform. Subsequent columns are independent and enter at physical
height one; selected projections are then retained. All horizontal axes are
already present. This differs from restarting a proof at an already selected
later history and from a subsequent tour with reused columns.

Initially the raw mark group is the full quotient

    H=Z^505/L_A ≅ F_q^400,

by the surjective map (x,y) -> x-Ay mod q. It is also generated by the old output
coordinate marks, so agrees with the fresh-column mark group in
[First tour](wall1_first_tour.md). Every mark has a nearest integer representative.
A mark is hit at radius R only if at least one integer point of B_505(R) maps
to it. Therefore, for **every** A,

    1-p_A(R) <= # (Z^505 intersect B_505(R)) / q^400.              (1)

Collisions among representatives only reduce the number of hit marks. There
is no averaging over A, no random-lattice hypothesis, and no need to assign
independent probabilities to lattice points in this argument.

## Explicit deterministic small-radius bound

Put n=505. Unit cubes centered at distinct integer points have disjoint
interiors. Those centered in B_n(R) lie in B_n(R+sqrt(n)/2). Hence

    #(Z^n intersect B_n(R)) <= V_n (R+sqrt(n)/2)^n.               (2)

Alternatively, the half of each cube satisfying <z,x-z><=0 lies in
B_n(sqrt(R^2+n/4)), giving the stronger bound

    #(Z^n intersect B_n(R)) <= 2 V_n (R^2+n/4)^(n/2).             (3)

The origin's cube contributes at least a half cube too. These are ordinary
Euclidean volume bounds on integer points, valid at the small radius in question.
No design-radius probability estimate is extrapolated to this radius.

For L_min=1.10*32.42764=35.670404,

    R_min=35.65638402198428... .

The base-ten logarithms of the right sides of (2)/q^400 and (3)/q^400 are
respectively enclosed around

    -938.000033986416818...,
    -987.394796252407597... .

In particular, using the conservative epsilon=10^(-987), (1) implies

    p_A(R_min)>1-epsilon,
    E_A[p_A(R_min)^24] >= 1-24 epsilon.                          (4)

The second inequality uses (1-epsilon)^24>=1-24 epsilon. This is a lower
bound on the **initial small-radius void moment**, not a lower bound on BKZ
failure. It shows that the desired tiny initial small-radius moment is false
at this initial state, rather than merely not proved by the current estimator.

## Consequence for the integrated potential and projection bound

The initial determinant is q^400. With the scale convention of the proposed
potential, g_0=(q^400/V_506)^(1/506), so

    g_0=3337.639980362230...,
    L_0=1.10*g_0=3671.403978398453... > L_min.

For d>0 and k=24, let Phi_d(0) be the proposed normalized integral. On
0<=t<=L_min its nonincreasing integrand f_0(t)^k is at least
p_A(R_min)^k. Thus pointwise

    Phi_d(0) >= (L_min/L_0)^d p_A(R_min)^k.                      (5)

Multiplying by the telescoping scale penalty (L_0/L_min)^d cancels that factor.
Consequently the sufficient first-exit upper-bound expression supplied by the
smoothed-potential argument, when initialized at this raw restart state, is
at least

    delta^(-24) * (1-24 epsilon).                               (6)

For delta=.18 or .280 this exceeds one. Clipping an upper bound at one gives
only a trivial result. This is a lower bound on the proposed *upper-bound
expression*, not on the actual exit probability. Changing d does not repair
this fixed-radius initialization route, because the cancellation in (5) is exact.

Likewise, repeated zero-layer projection gives p_i(R_min)<=p_0(R_min), but the
right side is almost one. It gives no useful small upper bound from (4).
Projection can greatly decrease the void, so the reverse inequality is not
available and (4) must not be attributed to any later selected state.

The smoothed note's corridor example instead begins around draw129, with
L_max=1.10*583.27530. That smaller L_max is not valid for the raw initial state
above. If starting at draw129, one must establish its initial integrated moment
under the actual selected-prefix law, or obtain quantitative gains from the
preceding 128 projections. A projected short representative can have a long
raw lift; the raw point-count upper bound (1) cannot upper-bound the number
of projected hits. Thus neither transferring the raw near-one lower bound nor
transferring a much-larger-radius small-void estimate is justified there.

## Reproduce the interval calculation

The following uses the elementary recurrence for Euclidean ball volumes and
outward interval arithmetic. It does not evaluate any selected history.

```python
from mpmath import iv
iv.dps=60
I=iv.mpf
q,m,n=3329,400,505
V=[I(1),I(2)]
for j in range(2,507):
    V.append(2*iv.pi*V[j-2]/j)
Lmin=I('1.10')*I('32.42764')
R=iv.sqrt(Lmin**2-1)
cube=(iv.ln(V[n])+n*iv.ln(R+iv.sqrt(I(n))/2)-m*iv.ln(q))/iv.ln(10)
half=(iv.ln(2)+iv.ln(V[n])+I(n)/2*iv.ln(R**2+I(n)/4)-m*iv.ln(q))/iv.ln(10)
assert cube<I(-938)
assert half<I(-987)
g0=iv.exp((m*iv.ln(q)-iv.ln(V[506]))/506)
assert I('3337.63998036222')<g0<I('3337.63998036224')
print('Lmin, R, initial L:',Lmin,R,I('1.10')*g0)
print('log10 coverage upper bounds:',cube,half)
```

No production file was modified and no new failure-probability estimate is
claimed. The initial-state obstruction is unconditional; the later selected
initialization remains open.
