# A nonorthogonal marked-Gram certificate for one-step history control

The multheight argument extends from one rectangle to states satisfying a
full Gram-matrix upper bound. This gives a uniform conditional certificate
on an explicit state family. However, the parameters below are impossible for actual cubic-projection
BKZ states, as proved below. This is an abstract local result only.

## State hypotheses fixed before the fresh mark

Let M subset K be full lattices in the incoming505-dimensional space. Let
q=3329 and choose400 vectors t_i in K with q t_i in M, inducing a surjection

    f:F_q^400 -> K/M,   a -> Σ_i a_i t_i modulo M.

The labels may be redundant. The generators, the quotient map, and all
bounds must be measurable from the entire incoming history, before the
next fresh mark. Require its conditional law to be uniform on K/M. The
algorithm appends a unit vertical coordinate, selects an actual shortest
nonzero graph vector, and uses the previously specified radius law.
Assume incoming scale g>=549 and the full Loewner inequality

    Gram(t_1,...,t_400) <= (102²/q²) I_400.                 (1)

Bounds on diagonal entries alone do not imply(1). This is a strong geometric
condition, not a property established for typical or all selected states.

For each label a choose y(a)=Σ_i center(a_i)t_i. It is a legitimate marked
representative, not necessarily a nearest one. By(1),

    ||y(a)||² <= 102² Σ_i center(a_i)²/q².                 (2)

At least one generator is nonzero if K/M is nontrivial. Then q t_i is a
nonzero horizontal vector in every appended graph lattice, of length at
most102. Therefore its actual shortest winner has s<=102. No separate
winner-cap hypothesis is required. If all generators vanish, the marked
group and the outgoing marked group are trivial and the void is zero.
The universal vertical comparison(0,q) also exists but is not needed here.

Consequently every branch has outgoing radius squared at least

    B0²=(1.10*549)² 102^(−2/506)−1 > 0.                   (3)

## Lifts, redundant labels, and factorization

Uniform labels in F_q^400 push forward to uniform quotient marks because
a surjective finite-group homomorphism has equal fibers. This applies to
both the fresh mark U and the auxiliary test mark V. For every integer j,

    y(V+jU)−k_f(V)−j k_f(U) belongs to M.

Thus (y(V+jU),j) represents the correct outgoing coset before projection.
The actual winner may depend on U; projection contractivity and(3) hold
pointwise, so no independence for that winner is introduced. A second
surjective map onto the projected marked group again has equal fibers.
A finite witness set itself need not be fiber-saturated; its inclusion in
the pulled-back true hit set suffices.

Let J={−16,...,16}, theta=1/10, ellbar=102 and
Ktheta(u)=q⁻¹Σ_v exp[theta Σ_(j in J) center(v+ju)²/q²].
Using(2) in the same simultaneous-miss Chernoff bound gives

    E[(P'_U)^24 | incoming history]
      <= exp[(24theta/ellbar²)(Σ_j j²−33 B0²)]
         * (q⁻¹Σ_u Ktheta(u)^24)^400.                    (4)

Factorization comes from the scalar majorant and independent uniform label
coordinates, not orthogonal geometry. All24 auxiliary replicas share the
same fresh label U. There is no kernel correction factor.

## Reachability obstruction at every draw

An actual state has K=P Z^N, generated by the projected coordinate vectors
P e_j of norm at most1. Write [K:M]=q^d, where d<=400, and use the incoming
scale convention det M=V_506 g^506, V_506=pi^253/253!.
Choose d of the t_i whose classes form a basis of K/M. These vectors are
real-linearly independent: lattice coordinates turn any real dependence
into a rational one; clearing denominators and dividing by the gcd gives a
primitive integer relation, nonzero modulo q, contradicting the chosen
quotient basis. Extend them to505 real-independent vectors using the unit
bounded generators P e_j. Hadamard's inequality and the sublattice index give

    det K <= (102/q)^d,
    det M = q^d det K <= 102^d <= 102^400.

This also holds for d=0 by extending the empty set. Therefore

    g <= (102^400 / V_506)^(1/506) < 212.241,

contradicting g>=549. The contradiction has an exact integer certificate:

    3^253 * 549^506 > 253! * 102^400,

using pi>3. It applies at every draw, independently of the earlier
rank-drop bound. A gate requiring this family already fails at its initial
actual state; no small exceptional-probability estimate can repair it.

The fixed rectangle from Part446 is an abstract member of this family.
It too is unreachable: its105 unmarked axes have spacing c>98, so all its
vectors of norm at most1 lie in the400-dimensional marked subspace. Such
vectors cannot generate its505-dimensional K. Its strict local contraction
remains valid, but is not a reachable-history example. Further work must
retain the cubic-projection geometry when designing representative bounds.
This obstruction does not disprove the BKZ target or rule out nonlinear
choices of marked representatives.
