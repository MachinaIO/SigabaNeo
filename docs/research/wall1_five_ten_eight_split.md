# Rare norm-eight pairs for five-circuit ten-position planes

After excluding the reduced(8,8) case for four-circuit planes, the largest
size17 stratum is rank8/girth5. Its largest two-relation term has t5 and
overlap4. The no-six family dominates that kernel, while the one-six
contribution is about10^-14 of the selected kernel.

In girth five a balanced integer norm-eight line has either eight unit
entries (four of each sign) or support five with shape(2,1,-1,-1,-1),
up to sign and permutation. The remaining balanced shape(2,-2) violates
girth five. Counting lines up to sign gives

    L = 35 choose(10,8) + 20 choose(10,5) = 6615.

There are at most choose(L,2)=21875805 planes generated by two independent
such lines. This is a witness upper bound, not an exact count of eligible
planes. It intentionally includes pairs with the wrong first support,
wrong second support, or smaller girth. Any independent pair has a nonzero
integer two-by-two minor of absolute value at most8, hence remains
independent modulo q=3329. Its span specifies at most one normalized
coefficient chart for each fixed pair of prescribed circuit supports.
Thus divide this witness bound by the existing coefficient count Q, using
min(Q,choose(L,2)). No saturation claim is needed: the existing full
no-six kernel already bounds every plane in the exceptional family.

If the reduced first and second squared minima are both8, charge the
plane to that exceptional family using the complete old GS kernel(8,6).
Otherwise the first squared minimum8 forces the second squared norm>=10,
giving second squared GS length>=10-4^2/8=8. If the first minimum is>=10,
use the general Gauss bounds(10,15/2). Consequently the replacement is

    min(1,choose(L,2)/Q) K(8,6)^N + K(8,8)^N + K(10,15/2)^N.

Here every K includes its cutoff14 complementary tail and periodic terms;
the notation specifies squared GS lower bounds. Take the smaller of this
sum and the previous complete no-six bound. All norm-six classes and
coefficient weights outside this split remain unchanged. Overcounting
between the three cases is harmless for an upper bound.

The exact script checks all6615 line representatives, their supports,
balance, squared norms, and sign normalization. It does not enumerate
21875805 pairs or claim that they are distinct eligible planes. The
classification and counting argument above supply the mathematical
coverage. This result is not yet formalized in Lean and does not close
the full size17..24 residual or adaptive BKZ distribution bridge.
