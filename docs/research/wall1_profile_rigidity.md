# A full-HKZ obstruction to uniform BKZ profile rigidity

Use zero-based indexing, covolume one, natural logarithms, and
$P_j=\sum_{i<j}\log g_i$. Put $b=\beta$, $k=N-b$. For the straight-line GSA convention put
$$
d_b=\frac{\log(b/(2\pi e))+b^{-1}\log(\pi b)}{2(b-1)},\qquad
F_j=j(N-j)d_b .
$$
Then $G=\exp(F_{k+1}-F_k)$ is the terminal GSA reference. These are definitions of the comparison curve. They impose no assumption on random lattice shapes.

## The two-sided target fails on Haar input

**Theorem.** Let $b/N\to a\in(0,1)$. With probability tending to one, the supremum over fully BKZ-$b$ reduced bases of $|P_k-F_k|/(N\log N)$ is at least
$(1-a+a\log a)/2-o(1)$.

**Proof.** Take a size-reduced full HKZ basis. At each start its first projected vector minimizes length in the entire projected lattice. Every shorter window spans a sublattice containing this vector, so the same minimum is attained there. This verifies all windows, including truncated ones. Proposition 3 for quasi-HKZ applies to this basis and gives $P_k/(N\log N)=-a\log(a)/2+o(1)$ on its high-probability event. Meanwhile $Nd_b/\log N\to1/(2a)$, hence $F_k/(N\log N)\to(1-a)/2$. Their difference is positive since $1-a+a\log a$ decreases to zero on $(0,1]$. The same obstruction applies to envelopes differing from $F_k$ by $o(N\log N)$. [Laarhoven, Proposition 3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15265v1)

## Finite-rank obstruction and terminal certificates

There is also a deterministic lower bound on the error required at rank 1023. In the full HKZ witness, with $h=N-j$, the quotient covolume is $e^{-P_j}$. Blichfeldt gives $\log g_j\le c_h-P_j/h$ with $c_h=\frac12\log(2/\pi)+h^{-1}\log\Gamma(h/2+2)$. Thus $P_k\le U_k$ where $U_0=0$ and $U_{j+1}=(1-1/h)U_j+c_h$, by induction. This holds for every unimodular lattice, including normalized q-ary inputs. The third column is a lower bound on the necessary two-sided error at this one prefix. Zero denotes the trivial bound. [Blichfeldt bound in the proof frontier](../PROOF_GAP.md)

|406|817.639|167.696|0.005933200|0.369|

|647|679.503|7.051|0.039603438|1.822|

The probability in column four is per width and simultaneous over bases. The figures come from the existing A6 integration. The computation below certifies the new numerical comparisons only. [A6 certificates](wall1_poisson_exponent.md)

```python
from mpmath import iv
iv.dps = 45
n = 1023
for b, upper, floor, target in [
    (406, "817.639", "167.696", ".369"),
    (506, "795.242", "86.234", ".816"),
    (647, "679.503", "7.051", "1.822"),
    (800, "461.111", "0", "3.413"),
]:
    k = n-b
    d = (iv.ln(b/(2*iv.pi*iv.exp(1))) + iv.ln(iv.pi*b)/b)/(2*(b-1))
    u = iv.mpf(0)
    for h in range(n,b,-1):
        c = iv.ln(2/iv.pi)/2 + iv.loggamma(iv.mpf(h)/2+2)/h
        u = (1-iv.mpf(1)/h)*u + c
    assert abs(u-iv.mpf(upper)) < iv.mpf(".001")
    if floor != "0":
        assert k*b*d-u > iv.mpf(floor)
    assert abs(iv.mpf(".85")*iv.exp((n-2*k-1)*d)-iv.mpf(target)) < iv.mpf(".001")
```

## Why the unrestricted primitive-flag moment diverges

For a primitive flag $W\subset S\subset L$ of rank difference $b$, let $T=\pi_{W^\perp}S$. A scale-invariant short-vector deficit for a shortest first vector in this block is
$D=\log(\det(T)^{1/b}/\lambda_1(T))$. Adding a dimension-dependent GH normalization changes it by a constant. Choosing a shortest primitive line in $T$ realizes the block SVP condition.

**Lemma.** For $2\le b<N$ the supremum of $D$ over such flags is infinite on every lattice, even when $S=L$ and $W$ has the terminal prefix rank $k=N-b$.

**Proof.** Fix a basis $v_1,\ldots,v_N$. Take $W_m$ generated by $v_1,\ldots,v_{k-1},mv_k+v_{k+1}$. This is primitive by unimodular completion with $v_k$. Its determinant grows linearly in $m$ asymptotically by multilinearity of the exterior product. The primitive extension by $v_k$ has fixed determinant $C$, since its span as a lattice is the first $k+1$ basis vectors. Its projected height is $C/\det W_m$. As $\det T_m=1/\det W_m$, we have $D\ge(1-1/b)\log\det W_m-\log C\to\infty$. A shortest first vector extends to a full HKZ tail. This proves divergence even with every internal tail test imposed. In particular the exponential half-moment of this unrestricted maximum is infinite. A Rogers or Schmidt averaging identity cannot give it a finite bound. The example does not verify crossing windows or prefix reduction.

## What remains open

Writing $E_j=P_j-F_j$ gives the exact identity $\log(g_k/G)=E_{k+1}-E_k$. Even a hypothetical uniform $|E_j|\le s$ would only imply $g_k/G\ge e^{-2s}$ directly. The sufficient tolerance for 0.85 by that inference is $s\le-\log(.85)/2$, about 0.08126. Large common errors can cancel. Thus the full HKZ witness disproves the proposed profile theorem without disproving the one-sided terminal conjecture. The adjacent floor $g_k\ge(\sqrt3/2)g_{k-1}$ still requires control of the preceding length to yield an absolute radius.

A moment over flags *completable to fully reduced bases* could exploit the crossing constraints. Its terminal deficit and adjacent prefix increments at the four widths remain uncontrolled at the requested scale. No improved Haar radius or q-ary probability transfer follows here. The observed local dip concerns the best bases found by a finite search and does not constrain all fully reduced bases.
